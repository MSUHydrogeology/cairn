## Options

| Route | Benefit / cost |
|---|---|
| Retain separate publication and installed operation | Keeps citation stability and a methodological landmark. Could reduce duplicate prescriptions without relocating; doing nothing retains repeated editing. |
| Public methodology under `docs/` | Groups explanation/reference with reader guides while installed operation stays explicit. Costs citation migration and the old landmark; a rename alone saves no edits. |
| Common methodology in `dev/rules/` | Groups prescriptions but conflates public product with project-local ownership; current delivery would need redesign. |
| Generated presentations | Might enforce parity but adds unearned authoring machinery. Not the accepted default. |

## Recommendation

Proposed paths:
- `docs/methodology/` — public explanation/reference after C2.

Choose the second route, **with the two explicit conflict dispositions above and preservation of partial duties as prerequisites to affected cuts**. Keep public rationale, useful specifications, framework duties and historical evidence. Give each operating requirement one complete usable installed owner; retain concise task-specific template fields and visible safety minima. Preserve deliberate adopter differences and project-owned `dev/rules/`. No generator is needed.

Public explanations should point to operative source/templates instead of maintaining another requirement table. What is lost is the old landmark/citation stability and, only if explicitly chosen, the two conflicting published prescriptions. No duty may disappear through a wording cleanup. C3 must repeat both baseline measurements and demonstrate an actual simpler edit without lost reach or meaning; predicted fewer presentations are not measured savings.
