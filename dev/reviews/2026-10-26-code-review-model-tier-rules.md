**B2 -- Blocking. R2's shortening dropped the sentence that makes the below-floor flag checkable, and nothing else ships it. (Restore it or get the Maintainer's allowance.)**
The Revision's R2 (the text the Decision accepted) said "Author and reviewer names carry the model." The implementation dropped it, with three other clauses, to fit the 8,520 figure. The plan's scope rule says accepted scope is a floor and a lighter mechanism owes nothing only if every accepted outcome is still delivered; the outcome "a reviewer can see work written below its floor" is not delivered by R2 as shipped. C2.4 authorized shortening, but not removing the one clause the flag depends on, and the Maintainer has not allowed the loss.
*Fix (recommended):* put the clause back and pay for it by removing the unverified clause "; an unset spawn inherits yours" (N1). Measured with tiktoken `cl100k_base`: the dropped clause costs 8 tokens and the inherits clause 6, so R2 and `AGENTS.md` end 2 tokens higher (8,520, the ceiling). Satisfier: the clause in both copies plus the pin; or a Maintainer allowance recorded in the plan.
