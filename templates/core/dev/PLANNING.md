# {{project_name}} — development roadmap

`PLANNING.md` is the **roadmap board**: what is queued, active,
and recently completed. It is the execution board, not the
permanent decision archive — completed items scrub out once their
lasting truth has moved to the docs, the changelog, and git
history (see the goodnight routine in `AGENTS.md`).

`PLANNING.md` is a **shared communication file**: edited only at
defined serialization points — a lifecycle archive, the goodnight
routine, or explicit maintainer instruction — and referring to
proposals and plans by **filename stem**, not path link, so an
archive-move needs no edit here.
